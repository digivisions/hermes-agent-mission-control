RealXR is Digital Visions' own premium, character-driven XR experience product (working directory RealXR_Character on Andy's Mac). It is sold through video: reels and scripted CGI/VFX scenes, mostly limbo-space product shots of the XR glasses.
§
There is ONE canonical reference character and every visual must use her: a sharp-featured Vietnamese woman in the Veronica Ngo mould, gunmetal coat with a mandarin collar. CHARACTER_REFERENCE.png is the locked reference; Talent_Turnaround_Sheet.png, Talent_Expression_Sheet.png and Talent_Posing_Sheet.png exist for consistency, with Talent_FullBody_SteelCoatV2.png and Talent_FullBody_FinalV2.png as full-body references. Two earlier casting options were explored and NOT chosen — a Jung Ho-yeon style and an edgy K-pop style. Do not reintroduce them.
§
The product prop is defined in Prop_RealXR_Glasses_DetailSheet.png and Prop_XR_Glasses_DesignV2.png. V2 supersedes V1 — always anchor to V2.
§
Environment and mood references exist for a cyberpunk city scene (Scene_Cyberpunk_City.png, RealXR_Prop_Cyberpunk.png), alongside raw reference photography for the talent and the glasses.
§
Current phase (2026-08): script and promo video production. A 15-second TVC script (RealXR_TVC_Script_15s.md), a treatment deck (treatment_deck.html) and a storyboard directory exist. Product-pitch documents and video scripts live at the root of the working directory; generated frames accumulate in a _gen subfolder.
§
Standing rule: when asked for RealXR visuals, ALWAYS anchor to the reference character and the V2 prop sheet. NEVER generate a new face. There is no code repository, no CLAUDE.md and no project memory for RealXR — this note plus the asset sheets are the whole context, so do not claim to have read files that do not exist.
